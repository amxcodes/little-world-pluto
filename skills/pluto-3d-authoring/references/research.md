# Research notes

- Three.js scene graphs informed the use of local origins, inherited transforms, and separate pivots. The animation manual informed the separation of property tracks and timing. The geometry docs distinguish profiled extrusions and axial forms. [Scene graph](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/scenegraph.html) · [Animation system](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/animation-system.html) · [ExtrudeGeometry](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/ExtrudeGeometry.html) · [LatheGeometry](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/LatheGeometry.html)
- Blender's keyframe workflow informed the distinction between a planned pose and its interpolated transition. Fusion's joint and interference tools informed separate checks for limits, contact and clearance. Pluto's checks do not establish CAD or simulation results. [Blender keyframes](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/2.90/animation/keyframes/introduction.html) · [Fusion interference inspection](https://help.autodesk.com/view/fusion360/ENU/?contextId=DESIGN-INSPECT-INTERFERENCE-CMD)
- Public Three.js skills separate animation, geometry, and visual QA. The community demonstration and its repository informed the use of rendered evidence to check the work. The Pluto instructions use its own recipe format and review flow.
